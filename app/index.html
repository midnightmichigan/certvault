How this is used

Since July 8, 2026, importers of CPSC-regulated products must eFile certificate data through CBP's ACE system before goods clear customs. Send this CSV to your customs broker — they map these fields into the PGA message set on the entry filing. CertVault generates the data format; your broker (or you as importer of record) remains the filer. CertVault is not a law firm, not a testing laboratory, and not a customs broker. Not legal advice — confirm requirements with the CPSC Small Business Ombudsman, your lab, or qualified counsel.
