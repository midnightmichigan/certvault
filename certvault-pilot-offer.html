FOUNDING PILOT — 10 SPOTS, THEN IT CLOSES

Your import certificates, organized and filing-ready for CPSC eFiling.

CPSC now requires certificate data for imported regulated products to be filed electronically through CBP's ACE system (16 CFR 1110). Paper certificates in a drawer are no longer enough. CertVault keeps every certificate, test report, and product record structured, searchable, and ready to file — so a customs question never becomes a customs hold.

Who this is for: small sellers importing children's products, toys, baby goods, or other regulated consumer products into the US and selling on Amazon, Walmart Marketplace, or TikTok Shop. If that is not you, this page is not for you — and we will tell you that on the intro call rather than sell you something you don't need.
