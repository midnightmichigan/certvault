The problem

The mandate is real. The paperwork is a mess.

Since July 8, 2026, every importer of CPSC-regulated consumer products must electronically file certificate-of-compliance data through CBP's ACE system before goods clear customs — including de minimis shipments. And the marketplaces aren't waiting around either.

📦

The mandate changed the game

16 CFR 1110 now requires eFiling of certificate data for every regulated shipment. Your broker needs structured data fields — a zip of random PDFs won't cut it anymore.

🚨

Amazon demands with no warning

Amazon periodically demands Children's Product Certificates and lab reports per SKU — sometimes 9 CPCs at once — with short deadlines. Walmart and TikTok Shop run their own certificate gates.

🕸️

Spreadsheets and panic

Most small sellers track compliance in a spreadsheet, supplier email threads, and PDFs scattered across Google Drive. When a deadline hits, it's a midnight dig through folders.

⏱️

A 20-hour scramble is normal

A single compliance demand — 9 CPCs plus lab results — can cost roughly 20 hours to assemble. For a one-person operation, that's a lost week of the business.

An honest word on enforcement. We won't claim customs will seize your shipment tomorrow. The mandate is final and in force, and missing certificate data can trigger examinations and holds — but how strictly CBP is enforcing the requirement at the ports can shift, so confirm where things stand with your customs broker. Amazon, meanwhile, doesn't do warning phases — a listing suspension costs you sales today. CertVault is about reducing a real, growing risk — not selling fear.

The product

One vault. Every SKU. Always filing-ready.

CertVault is a per-SKU compliance vault built for small US marketplace sellers in regulated categories — children's products, toys, electronics, cosmetics, supplements, sporting goods. It's in beta now, and the prototype app is live to try.
