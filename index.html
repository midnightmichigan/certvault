The problem

The mandate is real. The paperwork is a mess.

Since July 8, 2026, every importer of CPSC-regulated consumer products must electronically file certificate-of-compliance data through CBP's ACE system before goods clear customs — including de minimis shipments. And the marketplaces aren't waiting around either.

📦

The mandate changed the game

16 CFR 1110 now requires eFiling of certificate data for every regulated shipment. Your broker needs structured data fields — a zip of random PDFs won't cut it anymore.

🚨

Amazon demands with no warning

Amazon periodically demands Children's Product Certificates and lab reports per SKU — sometimes 9 CPCs at once — with short deadlines. Walmart and TikTok Shop run their own certificate gates.

🕸️

Spreadsheets and panic

Most small sellers track compliance in a spreadsheet, supplier email threads, and PDFs scattered across Google Drive. When a deadline hits, it's a midnight dig through folders.

⏱️

A 20-hour scramble is normal

A single compliance demand — 9 CPCs plus lab results — can cost roughly 20 hours to assemble. For a one-person operation, that's a lost week of the business.

An honest word on enforcement. We won't claim customs will seize your shipment tomorrow — that isn't happening yet. CBP is currently in a warning phase: entries with missing certificate data get flagged, not rejected. But the mandate is final, examinations and holds are rising, and Amazon doesn't do warning phases — a listing suspension costs you sales today. CertVault is about reducing a real, growing risk — not selling fear.

The product

One vault. Every SKU. Always filing-ready.

CertVault is a per-SKU compliance vault built for small US marketplace sellers in regulated categories — children's products, toys, electronics, cosmetics, supplements, sporting goods.

1

Certificate library per SKU / variant

Every CPC, GCC, test report, SDS, and lab accreditation letter stored per SKU and variant — parsed and searchable. No more digging through Drive at midnight.

2

Expiry & retest alerts at 90 / 60 / 30 days

Get warned before a certificate or test report goes stale — plus annual retest nudges. Never get caught with an expired CPC again.

3

CPC / GCC generator

Turn a test report plus your product data into a properly formatted Children's Product Certificate or General Certificate of Conformity — the document sellers get wrong most often.

4

eFiling-ready CSV export

One-click export of certificate data in CPSC's PGA message-set format. Hand your broker a file they can actually file — not a zip of random PDFs.

5

Amazon, Walmart & TikTok Shop pre-checks

Each SKU mapped against each marketplace's document requirements for its category. See the gaps before a suspension or a listing block finds them for you.
